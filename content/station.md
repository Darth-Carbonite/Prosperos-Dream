A sprawling, overcrowded wheel of a station where every corridor has a hustle and every breath has a price. Crews come aboard to trade, refuel, get patched up, or disappear.

**Station rules, as posted at every airlock:**

- Carry your O2 credstick at all times. It is your wallet, your passport and your locator, and the oxygen tax comes out of it daily.
- Hull-piercing weapons go in a rented locker.
- Stay out of unauthorized areas, *especially* The Choke.
